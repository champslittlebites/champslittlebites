Soaking & Souring Guide
Reduce anti-nutrients in grains, beans, nuts and seeds for better nutrient absorption
Grains, beans, legumes, nuts and seeds contain phytic acid — an anti-nutrient that blocks absorption of iron, zinc, calcium, magnesium, and manganese. Soaking and souring significantly reduces phytic acid and makes these foods much easier for baby to digest and absorb.
🌰 Nuts & Seeds
  1. Cover nuts and seeds with warm filtered water, fully submerged
  2. Add 1 tsp salt per 2 cups of nuts/seeds. Stir until dissolved
  3. Leave uncovered on counter for 12 hours or overnight
  4. Rinse in colander. Use as-is for nut butters or nut milks. Store in fridge up to 5 days
  5. OR dehydrate back to crunchy: spread on tray, bake at 150°F (70°C) for up to 24 hrs until completely dry
Remember: no whole nuts until baby's molars are in
🌾 Grains, Legumes & Beans
  1. Place dried grains, legumes, or beans in a glass bowl
  2. Cover with filtered water until fully submerged
  3. Add 1 tbsp culture/acid per 1 cup liquid: yogurt with live cultures, whey, kefir, kombucha, raw apple cider vinegar, or lemon juice
  4. Cover with a towel and place in a warm spot (pantry) for 12–24 hours
  5. Drain, rinse well, then cook. Soaked beans cook in 1–4 hours depending on type and age
Pre-soaked or sprouted grains are available at health food stores to save prep time
🥫 Which Foods Need Soaking?
Always Soak
Black beans · Chickpeas · Kidney beans · Lentils · Cannellini beans · Oats · Barley · Quinoa · Millet · Almonds · Walnuts · Cashews
Optional but Beneficial
Rice · Sunflower seeds · Pumpkin seeds · Hemp seeds · Chia seeds · Pine nuts · Flaxseeds
No Soaking Needed
Fresh fruits · Vegetables · Meat & poultry · Fish & seafood · Eggs · Dairy · Avocado · Banana

💡 Freezer Tips
🫙
Ice Cube Tray Method
Each cube = ~1 oz. Transfer frozen cubes to labeled zip bags. Grab 1–2 cubes per meal.
🏷️
Label Everything
Name, date made, age stage. Painter's tape + marker. Oldest always in front.
🔄
Thaw Safely
Overnight in fridge or sealed bag in warm water. Never refreeze thawed baby food.

🍼 Starting Solids — The Basics
⏰
Timing
Offer solids when baby is alert and happy. Mid-morning works well. Start with 1–2 teaspoons only.
🥄
Texture Progression
6 mo: smooth purée → 7–8 mo: lumpy/mashed → 9–10 mo: soft chunks → 11–12 mo: soft table food
💧
Hydration
Breast milk or formula remains primary until 12 months. Small sips of water at meals is fine after 6 months.
