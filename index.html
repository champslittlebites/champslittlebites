💡 Freezer Tips
🫙
Ice Cube Tray Method
Freeze purées in ice cube trays (each cube ≈ 1 oz). Transfer to labeled zip bags. Grab 1–2 cubes per meal.
🏷️
Label Everything
Label with: food name, date made, age stage. Painter's tape + marker works great. Oldest in front!
🔄
Thaw Safely
Thaw overnight in fridge or in warm water. Never refreeze thawed baby food. Discard uneaten within 24 hrs.

🍼 Starting Solids — The Basics
⏰
Timing
Offer solids when baby is alert and happy — mid-morning often works best. Start with 1–2 teaspoons.
🥄
Texture Progression
6 mo: smooth purée → 7–8 mo: mashed/lumpy → 9–10 mo: soft chunks → 11–12 mo: small soft table food
💧
Hydration
Breast milk or formula remains primary until 12 months. Small sips of water with meals fine after 6 months.

💰 Budget Grocery Tips
🥕
Best Budget Produce
Sweet potato, butternut squash, banana, apple, pear, avocado, carrots — all under $1/serving and highly nutritious.
🏪
Where to Shop
Aldi & Trader Joe's for organic produce · Costco for oats, chicken, avocados · Frozen veggies = same nutrition, half the price.
📅
Seasonal Savings
Frozen peas, corn, and spinach are always affordable. Buy fresh squash and sweet potatoes in fall/winter.
