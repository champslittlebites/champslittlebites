🍼 Starting Solids
Timing
Offer solids when baby is alert and happy. Mid-morning works best. Start with just 1–2 teaspoons.
Texture Progression
6 mo: smooth purée → 7–8 mo: mashed → 9–10 mo: soft chunks → 11–12 mo: soft table food
Hydration
Breast milk or formula is primary until 12 months. Small sips of water with meals fine from 6 months.
